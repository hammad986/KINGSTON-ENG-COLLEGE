Why Partner With Us

What Kingston Brings to Your Talent Pipeline

Our graduates arrive pre-trained, technically sharp, and culturally fit for corporate environments — reducing your onboarding time and cost.

Rigorous Pre-Placement Training
Students undergo 300+ hours of training including aptitude, logical reasoning, verbal ability, group discussions, mock interviews, and resume workshops — long before drives begin.
Technical & Domain Depth
Graduates hold Anna University degrees across CSE, IT, ECE, AI&DS, AIML, CSBS, MECH, Arch, & MBA. Labs, live projects, internships, and value-added courses ensure hands-on readiness.
AMCAT / eLitmus Certified
Students are coached and certified through AMCAT, eLitmus, and CoCubes platforms — giving you standardised aptitude scores to filter candidates before the drive.
Dedicated Drive Coordination
Our Placement & Training Cell handles everything: hall booking, student shortlisting, communication, PPT logistics, interview scheduling, and post-offer follow-up at zero cost to you.
13 Years of Trusted Partnership
Companies like TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Cognizant, and Accenture have recruited from Kingston consistently for 3–5 years, a testament to the quality and reliability of our talent pool.
Strategic Location — Vellore
Vellore is 140 km from Chennai and 210 km from Bengaluru, making Kingston easily accessible for both virtual and on-campus drives from all major hiring hubs.
How It Works

Campus Placement Process — Step by Step

A streamlined, transparent process from company registration to joining — managed end-to-end by our Placement & Training Cell.

Phase 1
Company Registration & JD Submission
HR / Talent Acquisition teams register via our online form or contact the Placement Cell directly. Job Descriptions are shared specifying role, eligibility criteria (CGPA, arrear policy), CTC, and location. The Placement Cell confirms dates and logistics within 5 working days.
JD Submission Drive Date Confirmation 5-Day Turnaround

Phase 2
Student Shortlisting & Pre-Placement Preparation
The Placement Cell shortlists eligible students per your criteria (branch, CGPA, active backlogs). A Pre-Placement Talk (PPT) is scheduled — in-person or virtual. Students undergo targeted mock tests, GD sessions, and technical refreshers aligned to your JD.
Eligibility Screening PPT Session JD-Aligned Prep
Sep –
Nov
Phase 3 · IT Services & Consulting
Online / Proctored Aptitude & Coding Test
A proctored online test (Aptitude + Coding / Case Study) is conducted on-campus using company-provided or third-party platforms (AMCAT, HackerRank, Mercer Mettl). Our IT lab accommodates 200+ candidates simultaneously.
200-Seat IT Lab Proctored Environment HackerRank / AMCAT
Oct –
Dec
Phase 3 · Finance & Core Engineering
Domain Test & Group Discussion
For Finance (BFSI) and Core Engineering sectors, domain-specific written tests, case-based group discussions, and portfolio reviews are arranged in our seminar halls. MBA, ECE, and Mechanical stream students are especially prepared for BFSI and manufacturing roles.
Group Discussion Domain Test Mfg & BFSI Ready
Nov –
Jan
Phase 4
Technical & HR Interview Rounds
Multi-round interviews are conducted on-campus or via video link. The Placement Cell arranges interview rooms, schedules candidates in batches, and manages panel logistics. Virtual drives are equally supported with Zoom / Teams facilitation.
Interview Rooms Virtual Drive Support Batch Scheduling

Apr
Phase 5
Offer Letters, Verification & Joining Support
Offer letters are coordinated through the Placement Cell. Document verification (mark sheets, certificates, ID proof) is handled on-campus. Students receive pre-joining guidance. Our cell maintains a high offer-acceptance rate — ensuring your recruited pool actually shows up on Day 1.
