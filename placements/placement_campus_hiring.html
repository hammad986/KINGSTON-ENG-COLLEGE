Campus Hiring

Campus Hiring at KEC

Kingston Engineering College invites leading companies across sectors to conduct campus recruitment drives directly on campus. Our Placement Cell coordinates end-to-end with recruiters — from Pre-Placement Talks to offer letter dispatch — ensuring a smooth, professional experience for both companies and students. With a strong industry network built over two decades, KEC consistently delivers placement-ready graduates who contribute from day one.

Campus Hiring Process

1

Job Notification

Company shares JD, eligibility criteria, and preferred dates with the Placement Cell. Details are published on the student portal.

2

Pre-Placement Talk

Company representatives conduct a PPT to brief students about the organisation, role, growth path, and selection process.

3

Aptitude & Written Test

Shortlisting round assessing quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning, verbal ability, and domain-specific technical knowledge.

4

Technical Interview

In-depth interview on core engineering concepts, programming, and problem-solving relevant to the role applied for.

5

HR Interview

Final round evaluating communication, attitude, cultural fit, career goals, and salary expectations before offer rollout.

6

Offer Letter

Selected students receive offer letters through the Placement Cell. Documentation and joining formalities are facilitated on campus.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Minimum 60% aggregate (6.0 CGPA) in B.E. / B.Tech throughout (X, XII & UG)
  • No active backlogs at the time of campus drive registration
  • Final-year or pre-final year students as per company requirement
  • Valid government-issued ID and updated resume submitted before the PPT date
  • Students who have not yet accepted any other offer (One-offer policy applicable)
