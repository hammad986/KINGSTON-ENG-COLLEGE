Alumni Association

Reconnecting & Growing Together

About

To bring all the Alumnis of Kingston Engineering College under one vibrant forum for the exchange of experience and knowledge and develop a strong network among themselves for bonding and fellowship and their career advancement.

Distinguish Alumni Award

A distinguished Alumni award is given to the Alumni in the following categories for their excellence:

  • Eminent Scientist
  • Prominent Industrialist/Entrepreneur/Administrator
  • Philanthropists/Personnel who have rendered noteworthy social service
  • Young Alumnus Award in the field of Research/Entrepreneur/Sports/Arts/
  • Administrator
  • Eminent personnel in Sports/Arts/Music

Objectives of the Society

  • To conduct/sponsor Career Guidance Programme for students to succeed in interviews through training in core competence employability skills and soft skills training
  • To create new avenues for interaction of students, faculty with industry to encourage the development of employability skills through industrial visits and to identify industry-specific projects and prototype development
  • To assist disabled students with necessary gadgets, aids, counselling and special sessions
  • To conduct seminars, conference, meeting of professionals from all disciplines for the purpose of exchange and promotion of knowledge, among students, faculty and members of the Association
  • To provide a forum for the alumni for exchange ideas of academic, cultural and social issues of the day by organizing and coordinating reunion activities of the Alumni
  • This is always abide by Rules and Regulations
  • This Society is to renew for every year with product of the statement
  • This is non-consideration
  • This Society is free of Political Occurrence and also non profitable
