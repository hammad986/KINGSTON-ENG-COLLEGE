Milestones & History

Key achievements on Kingston's journey of excellence

2001
Institution Founded
Kingston Engineering College established in Vellore, Tamil Nadu with AICTE approval and affiliation to Anna University.
2010
Autonomous Status Granted
UGC granted autonomous status, enabling curriculum flexibility and enhanced academic control aligned with industry needs.
2018
NBA Accreditation — Cycle I
National Board of Accreditation granted accreditation to CSE, ECE, and Mechanical Engineering programmes for the first time.
2021
NAAC 'A' Grade
National Assessment and Accreditation Council awarded 'A' Grade with CGPA 3.07, a landmark achievement in quality assurance.
2022
NBA Accreditation — Cycle II
Renewed NBA accreditation for all eligible programmes, with additional departments receiving accreditation for the first time.
2024
3,499+ Dream Offers
Record-breaking placement year with 3,499+ dream and super-dream offers from top MNCs. Highest CTC of 10 LPA achieved.
