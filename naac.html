NAAC Assessment Criteria

Our institution is evaluated across 7 key criteria that encompass all aspects of academic and institutional quality.

Criterion 1 — Curricular Aspects

Covers curriculum design, development, and implementation including value-added courses and integration of industry needs.

  • 1.1 Curriculum Design
  • 1.2 Academic Flexibility
  • 1.3 Curriculum Enrichment
  • 1.4 Feedback System
Criteria 1.1 – 1.4 View Details

Criterion 2 — Teaching-Learning & Evaluation

Addresses student enrollment, teaching-learning processes, examination and evaluation methodologies.

  • 2.1 Student Enrolment
  • 2.2 Catering to Student Diversity
  • 2.3 Teaching-Learning Process
  • 2.4 Teacher Profile
Criteria 2.1 – 2.7 View Details

Criterion 3 — Research, Innovation & Extension

Covers research output, innovation ecosystem, consultancy, and institutional social responsibility activities.

  • 3.1 Resource Mobilization
  • 3.2 Innovation Ecosystem
  • 3.3 Research Publications
  • 3.4 Extension Activities
Criteria 3.1 – 3.5 View Details

Criterion 4 — Infrastructure & Learning Resources

Evaluates physical and academic infrastructure including library, IT, and support facilities for enhanced learning.

  • 4.1 Physical Facilities
  • 4.2 Library
  • 4.3 IT Infrastructure
  • 4.4 Maintenance
Criteria 4.1 – 4.4 View Details

Criterion 5 — Student Support & Progression

Addresses student support services including scholarships, career guidance, placements, and student activities.

  • 5.1 Student Support
  • 5.2 Student Progression
  • 5.3 Student Participation
  • 5.4 Alumni
Criteria 5.1 – 5.4 View Details

Criterion 6 — Governance, Leadership & Management

Evaluates institutional vision, leadership effectiveness, financial management, and human resource development.

  • 6.1 Institutional Vision
  • 6.2 Strategy Development
  • 6.3 Faculty Empowerment
  • 6.5 Internal Quality
Criteria 6.1 – 6.5 View Details

Criterion 7 — Institutional Values & Best Practices

Covers gender equity, environmental sustainability, inclusiveness, and institutional best practices and innovations.

  • 7.1 Institutional Values
  • 7.2 Best Practices
  • 7.3 Institutional Distinctiveness
Criteria 7.1 – 7.3 View Details

NAAC Accreditation Process

How Kingston Engineering College undergoes the NAAC quality assessment cycle.

1

Institutional Preparation

Data collection, self-assessment, and preparation of the SSR document

2

IIQA Submission

Submission of Institutional Information for Quality Assessment to NAAC

3

SSR Submission

Detailed Self Study Report submitted covering all 7 criteria

4

Peer Team Visit

Expert Peer Team visits the institution for on-site evaluation

5

Accreditation Award

NAAC awards the accreditation grade based on comprehensive assessment
