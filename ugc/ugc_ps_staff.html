Faculty & Staff

Kingston Engineering College maintains a qualified, experienced, and diverse teaching staff across all engineering, technology, and management disciplines. Faculty members hold doctoral and postgraduate qualifications from recognized universities, with expertise spanning teaching, research, and industry experience.

This disclosure presents the department-wise faculty profile including designation, highest qualification, area of specialisation, and teaching experience — in compliance with UGC Mandatory Disclosure norms. Non-teaching staff details covering technical, administrative, and support functions are also included.
