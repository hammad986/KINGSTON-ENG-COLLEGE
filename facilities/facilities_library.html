Guidelines & Governance

Library Policies & Conduct

01
Library Identity Card

All standard books are issued via Library Identity Cards. Cards are strictly non-transferable.

02
Book Renewal Policy

Borrowers may renew once if no reservation is active on the requested title.

03
Overdue Fine

Overdue fine of Rs. 1/- per day per book is levied consistently without exception.

04
In-House Reference Only

Journals, Magazines & Project Reports are for in-library reference only and cannot be issued.

01
Strict Silence Zone

Complete silence must be maintained within all library premises at all times.

02
Property Counter

Personal bags, files, and books must be deposited at the secure Property Counter before entry.

03
RFID Tag Integrity

Tampering or removing RFID tags will attract a penalty of Rs. 100/- per occurrence.

04
No Defacement

Scribbling, underlining, or any defacing of books will attract strict disciplinary action.

01
Immediate Reporting

Loss must be reported immediately to the Librarian to freeze accumulation of overdue fines.

02
Book Replacement

The borrower must provide a new copy of the same book or its latest edition as replacement.

03
300% Financial Penalty

A monetary fine of 3× the current market price of the lost book will be levied.

04
No-Dues Clearance

All outstanding fines must be settled before semester No-Dues clearance is issued.
