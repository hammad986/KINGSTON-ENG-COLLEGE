Network Infrastructure

Internet, Intranet & Computing

Internet & Intranet

A dedicated internet lab operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, providing free internet access to all students. The campus enjoys 1155 Mbps 1:1 dedicated leased line connectivity — 1 Gbps from Tata and 155 Mbps from Vodafone Idea. Internet is accessible from all 2000+ computers across every department.

Tata 1 Gbps Vodafone Idea 155 Mbps Fibre Optic Backbone 1:1 Dedicated Line

PCs & Laptops

The college maintains more than 2000 Desktop PCs / Laptops across various academic blocks, all interconnected through a fibre optic backbone. Full internet access is provided to every computer. All academic laboratories are equipped to support lab classes, research, training, and certification programs.

All Departments Fibre Optic Network
Software Policy

Licensed & Open-Source Software

Free & Open-Source (FOSS)

Kingston Engineering College has been migrating to Free and Open-Source Software (FOSS) for over 10 years. Most computers now run exclusively on open-source technologies. FOSS is deployed wherever it is a viable replacement for commercial alternatives, promoting digital sovereignty in education.

10+ Years FOSS Journey Open-Stack Technologies

Licensed Proprietary Software

Adequate licenses are maintained for proprietary software aligned with the curriculum. All labs are deployed with licensed software to support curriculum requirements, research, training, and certification programs. Proprietary tools are installed selectively to expose students to the latest industry technologies.

AICTE Compliant Fully Licensed
Wireless Connectivity

24×7 Campus-Wide Wi-Fi

Free Wi-Fi Across the Entire Campus

A free 24×7 Wi-Fi facility covers the entire campus including hostels, cafeteria, seminar halls, and the auditorium. Students and faculty can access the internet from anywhere within the campus. The campus intranet carries Test Schedules, Time Tables, Examination Schedules, Campus News, and a wealth of institutional information.

Hostels Cafeteria Seminar Halls Auditorium All Academic Blocks
Core Infrastructure

Data Centre & Network Security

Data Centre

A dedicated Data Centre spanning 900 sq.ft hosts all rack and blade servers with uninterrupted power supply (UPS), managed by the Centre for Technical Support. A 4 TB NAS storage system provides reliable data backup for all institutional operations.

900 sq.ft Facility Rack & Blade Servers 4 TB NAS Backup UPS Protected

Cyberoam 300ING Firewall

The Cyberoam 300ING Firewall handles enhanced network and application loads for both academic and administrative processes, providing a secure campus environment with deep packet inspection, content filtering, and threat management capabilities.

Deep Packet Inspection Content Filtering Threat Management
Smart Campus Systems

ICT, Biometrics & Technical Support

ICT-Enabled Classrooms

Classrooms and seminar halls are equipped with interactive panels, smartboards, and lecture capturing systems, as well as LCD projectors for rich multimedia delivery of all academic sessions.

Biometric & Barcode Systems

Biometric attendance systems are implemented campus-wide for all staff. Barcode scanners in the Library manage book issue/return and track entry/exit movement of students and faculty.

Centre for Technical Support (CTS)

All IT-related activities — network, hardware, software, interactive panels, projectors, UPS and general IT maintenance — are centrally managed by the dedicated CTS team.

Laboratory Facilities

Specialized Laboratories

FOSS Lab

Dedicated to Free and Open Source Software, featuring fully integrated Linux environments and open-stack technologies. Promotes academic freedom and digital sovereignty in technical education.

Enterprise Networking Lab

Equipped with Cisco Managed Switches and 24×7 Wi-Fi controllers for hands-on network administration training. Students gain real-world experience configuring enterprise-grade hardware.
