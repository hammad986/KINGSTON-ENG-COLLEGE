Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  |  Kingston Engineering College

1. Introduction

Kingston Engineering College ("we", "our", or "the College") is committed to protecting the privacy of all individuals who visit our website or interact with our institution. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information in accordance with applicable Indian data protection laws.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Identification: Name, email address, phone number, date of birth when you submit forms or apply for admission.
  • Academic Information: Educational qualifications, marks, and certificates submitted during the admission process.
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on our website (collected automatically).
  • Communication Data: Correspondence submitted via our contact or grievance forms.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Process admission applications and communicate with applicants.
  • Respond to enquiries and provide requested services.
  • Improve our website, courses, and institutional services.
  • Send important notices, academic calendars, and institutional updates.
  • Comply with legal obligations under AICTE, Anna University, and UGC regulations.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our website uses SSL encryption for all data transmissions.

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. These are small files stored on your device that help us remember your preferences and analyse website traffic. You may disable cookies through your browser settings; however, some features may not function correctly without them.

6. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with regulatory bodies (AICTE, Anna University, NAAC, UGC) as required by law, and with trusted service providers who assist in operating our website or conducting institutional activities, provided they agree to keep such information confidential.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and review the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.

8. Contact Us

For any privacy-related queries or to exercise your rights, please contact us at:
