Eligibility Criteria — UG Programmes

B.E. / B.Tech. Programmes

Passed 10+2 (HSC) or equivalent with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (PCM). Minimum 45% aggregate (40% for SC/ST candidates).

B.Arch. Programme

Passed 10+2 with Mathematics as compulsory subject. Minimum 50% aggregate marks. Must qualify in NATA (National Aptitude Test in Architecture).

Vocational Stream

Candidates from vocational stream with eligible subjects as per TNEA / Anna University norms. Bridge course may apply.

Lateral Entry Admission — B.E. / B.Tech.

Diploma holders and B.Sc. graduates can seek direct admission to the 3rd semester (2nd year) of B.E. / B.Tech. programmes through Tamil Nadu Engineering Admissions — Lateral Entry (TANCET / TNEA LE).

Diploma Holders

10+3 Diploma in relevant engineering branch with minimum 45% aggregate from a recognised State Board of Technical Education.

B.Sc. Graduates

B.Sc. (Physics / Mathematics / Computer Science) graduates with Mathematics at 10+2 level. Eligible for CSE and related programmes.

Admission Route

Through TNEA Lateral Entry (LE) counselling conducted by Anna University. Seats are reserved as per government norms (6.67% supernumerary).

Lateral Entry Process

Step 1

Register on the TNEA / Anna University portal for Lateral Entry admissions.

Step 2

Enter Kingston (Code: 1520) in your college preferences for your desired branch.

Step 3

On allotment, report to Kingston with original diploma/degree mark sheets and TC.

Step 4

Complete enrolment and pay fee. You join directly as a 2nd year student.

MBA Admissions — Masters in Business Administration

Kingston Engineering College offers a 2-year full-time MBA programme affiliated to Anna University. Admissions are through TANCET (Tamil Nadu Common Entrance Test) or management quota.

Eligibility

Any Bachelor's degree (10+2+3) from a recognised university with minimum 50% aggregate (45% for SC/ST). Final year students may also apply.

Entrance Exam

Valid TANCET MBA score is mandatory. CAT / MAT / CMAT scores may also be considered. Apply via Anna University's admission portal.

Programme Highlights

Associated with Madras Management Association (MMA). Industry expert lectures, workshops, and live projects. Strong placement record.

Duration & Mode

2 Years full-time (4 semesters). Includes summer internship. Campus: Chittoor Main Road, Vellore.

Documents Required for Admission

Please bring all originals along with 2 sets of self-attested photocopies at the time of reporting.

10th Mark Sheet

SSC / Matriculation Certificate (original + 2 copies)

12th Mark Sheet & Certificate

HSC / +2 mark sheet with passing certificate (original + 2 copies)

Transfer Certificate (TC)

Original TC from the last attended institution

Conduct Certificate

From the head of institution last attended

Community Certificate

For BC / MBC / SC / ST category candidates (original)

Income Certificate

For scholarship and fee concession applications (if applicable)

Passport Size Photographs

8 recent colour photographs (white background)

Aadhaar Card

Self-attested copy of Aadhaar card (mandatory)

TNEA Allotment Letter

Printed copy of the official TNEA seat allotment order

Medical Fitness Certificate

From a registered medical practitioner (for architecture/sports applicants)

Sports/Extracurricular Certificates

If applicable (for Sports quota applicants)

Anti-Ragging Undertaking

Signed undertaking from student and parent (provided at college)

Important: All documents must be submitted within 7 days of joining. Failure to submit original documents may result in cancellation of admission. Keep photocopies ready before reporting.

Scholarships & Financial Assistance

Kingston Engineering College ensures that financial constraints never come in the way of quality education. Various government and institutional scholarships are available.

🏛️

Full fee reimbursement for SC/ST/MBC students from families with annual income below ₹2.5 lakhs. Applied through scholarship.tn.gov.in

🇮🇳

National Scholarship Portal (NSP) schemes for minority communities, OBC, EWS. Includes Pre-Matric & Post-Matric scholarships.

💡

👩
